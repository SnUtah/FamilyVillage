Where the photos go

In config.js, change demoPhotos to false, then put photos in each theme folder in order: 1.jpg, 2.jpg ...
  trips     Family trips
  holidays  Holidays and birthdays
  growing   Kids growing up
  everyday  Ordinary days
  old       Old photos
Set each theme's count to the number of photos. Shrink them to about 1600px on the long side and the site loads faster.
