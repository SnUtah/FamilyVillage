🌿 Our family village

A family website: walk around a village of log cabins in the woods (🌿 spring‑summer · 🍂 autumn · ❄️ winter) as a character, answer the question of the day, swap letters and hearts and grow a tree together. Made for phones held upright, so on a PC it shows in a phone frame.

Controls
Click = walk there · Drag = joystick
W A S D move · E visit · Space jump
H heart (at a house: to that person) · N day/night · Esc close
